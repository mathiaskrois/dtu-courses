01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

k-D trees reduce neighbour-search work

Spatial partitioning helps answer nearest-neighbour queries. Dimensionality and data distribution influence how useful the index is.

02

ICP alternates two steps

Find correspondences under the current transform, then improve alignment. Repeat until a stopping condition; a local optimum need not be the correct match.

03

Initialisation and outliers matter

Poor overlap, symmetries and bad matches can defeat ICP. Inspect residuals and use a reasonable starting transform.

02 / PRACTISE

Trace one translation-only ICP step

30–60 min · Suggested

In 1D, source points are [0,2] and target points are [1,3]. Use nearest neighbours at the identity transform; break the tie for source 2 in favour of target 3. Find the mean matched displacement and updated source points.
