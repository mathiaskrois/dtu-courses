Topics

Delaunay is a geometric criterion

In 2D, no other input point lies strictly inside a triangle’s circumcircle. Cocircular points can produce non-unique triangulations.

Normals depend on neighbourhoods

Local geometry can estimate a surface normal, but noise, neighbourhood size and orientation affect the result.

Clean before interpreting

Outliers can distort triangulation and normal estimation. Inspect what filtering removes and whether useful detail is lost.

Check a circumcircle
