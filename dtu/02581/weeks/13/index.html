01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

Simplification spends an error budget

Fewer triangles improve cost, but feature preservation and topology matter. State what you aim to preserve before choosing an operation.

02

Mesh repairs have consequences

Closing holes or changing connectivity can alter the represented object. Inspect normals, boundaries and non-manifold edges afterwards.

03

Rendering should communicate evidence

Use lighting and viewpoints that reveal the geometry, including defects. A polished image is not a substitute for measuring approximation error.

02 / PRACTISE

Write a before-and-after audit

30–60 min · Suggested

A mesh goes from 20,000 to 5,000 triangles. Calculate the percentage reduction. Plan two geometric checks and a fair comparison rendering for your own assignment.
