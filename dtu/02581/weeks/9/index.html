Topics

Principal curvatures are directional extremes

Normal curvature depends on the tangent direction; Euler’s formula combines the two principal curvatures.

Geodesics stay on the surface

A geodesic is locally straight intrinsically. Its 3D appearance can be curved; local geodesics need not be globally shortest.

Approximation changes the path

An edge-only mesh path is constrained by triangulation. Distinguish it from an approximation allowed to cross faces.
