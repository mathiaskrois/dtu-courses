Surface reconstruction

Topics

A point cloud does not specify connectivity

Reconstruction must infer a surface from samples. Density, noise and missing regions limit what can be justified.

Normals provide orientation information

Oriented normals help determine an implicit field. Inconsistent orientation can produce a reconstruction with the wrong inside/outside structure.

Validate against the input

Compare the reconstructed surface with samples and inspect holes, oversmoothing and unexpected components. A visually clean result can hide lost detail.

Choose a reconstruction strategy

Optional practice

You have a point cloud sampled densely on one side of an object and sparsely on the other, with some flipped normals. Identify two checks before reconstruction and two checks afterwards.
