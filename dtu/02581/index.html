Keep rigid motion separate from changes to shape.

Understand

The essential ideas

    Centre corresponding points, estimate a rotation from their cross-covariance and recover translation.

  3. Coordinate conventions matter

    State whether points are rows or columns, the order of rotation and translation, and the frame used for each quantity..

Topology & curvature

Distinguish global topology from local bending.

Open week 8 page ↗Week page ↗
01

Understand

The essential ideas
  1. Euler characteristic checks connectivity

    For a closed orientable connected surface, V−E+F=2−2g.

  2. Gaussian curvature reflects two directions

    K=k₁k₂: positive for elliptic points, negative for saddle points and zero when one principal curvature vanishes..

  3. Discrete estimates need care

    Mesh resolution, irregular triangles and sign conventions affect curvature estimates.
