Attend your oral exam

From 8 DecemberIndividual slot to confirm · 2026 · Copenhagen
Open DTU Learn ↗ (opens a new tab)
Requirements & checklist

Intro slides announce the oral exam on 8 December plus one or two days. Your own date and time are not confirmed here.

  • Find your individual exam date, time and location in Learn or the official exam schedule.
  • Prepare to explain the geometry, algorithms and your submitted exercise work.
  • Use the reading list under Exam as a starting point; its filename still says 2025, so check for an updated version.
  • 8 December is the announced start of the exam period, not a confirmed appointment for every student.

Mesh Optimization and Visualization

9 December23:59 · 2026 · Copenhagen
Open DTU Learn ↗ (opens a new tab)
Requirements & checklist

Optimise meshes, then render the result in Blender.

  • Start from mesh_optim.ipynb and the supplied meshes or suitable meshes you find.
  • Reduce triangles while preserving quality; address holes or mesh structure where relevant.
  • Import your optimised mesh in Blender and produce a considered rendering.
  • Check the assignment in Learn for the required upload package; this folder accepts more than HTML.
Official requirements ↗ (opens a new tab)
Assessment & working requirements
ASSESSMENT

Assignments + an oral exam.

Work on the official notebooks in groups of 2–4 and submit completed work in Learn, normally as HTML. The grade uses both assignments and oral exam performance; no percentage split is stated in the intro slides.
