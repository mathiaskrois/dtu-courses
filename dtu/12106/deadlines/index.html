Task 1 · Problem formulation

30 September23:59 · 2026 · Copenhagen
Open in DTU Learn ↗ (opens a new tab)
Requirements & checklist

Define what you are assessing, why, and within which boundaries.

  • Introduce your microfabrication topic, context and motivation across environment, economy and society.
  • State the purpose and scope; define the object of assessment and a quantified functional unit.
  • Set system boundaries and explain assumptions.
  • One person submits for the whole group in FeedbackFruits.
  • Include a Table of Contributions agreed by every group member.
  • Default maximum: 7,200 characters (about 3 pages), excluding bibliography and tables; case-specific instructions take precedence.
  • Declare any use of language models. No specific document format is required.

Task 2 · Quantification

18 November23:59 · 2026 · Copenhagen
Open in DTU Learn ↗ (opens a new tab)
Requirements & checklist

Quantify impacts and discuss the balance between the three sustainability pillars.

  • Evaluate environmental, economic and social aspects; discuss trade-offs.
  • Where relevant and possible, calculate the carbon footprint of energy consumption or another relevant impact measure.
  • Put results in perspective using comparable processes or consumables; compare production and use where relevant.
  • One person submits for the whole group in FeedbackFruits.
  • Include a Table of Contributions agreed by every group member.
  • Attach Task 1 as an annex.
  • Default maximum: 9,600 characters (about 4 pages), excluding bibliography and tables; case-specific instructions take precedence.
  • Declare any use of language models. No specific document format is required.

Task 3 · Assessment & outlook

13 December23:59 · 2026 · Copenhagen
Open in DTU Learn ↗ (opens a new tab)
Requirements & checklist

Bring the project together: absolute sustainability, systemic effects, trade-offs and uncertainty.

  • Assess your share of safe operating space and the global significance of your case.
  • Discuss a systemic perspective, circular economy opportunities and trade-offs between sustainability dimensions.
  • Integrate Tasks 1 and 2 into a multidimensional assessment; conclude and assess uncertainties.
  • One person submits for the whole group in the Microfabrication Task 3 assignment tool.
  • Include a Table of Contributions agreed by every group member.
  • Attach both Task 1 and Task 2 as annexes.
  • Default maximum: 9,600 characters (about 4 pages), excluding bibliography and tables; case-specific instructions take precedence.
  • Declare any use of language models. No specific document format is required.
