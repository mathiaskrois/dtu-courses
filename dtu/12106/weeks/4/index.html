Topics

An inventory becomes an impact through factors

Multiply activity quantities by compatible impact factors and sum over the boundary. Check units carefully: kWh × kg CO₂e/kWh gives kg CO₂e, whereas a factor in grams needs conversion.

The comparison includes more than operation

The mobility exercise compares production and use, illustrating why a cleaner operating phase can coexist with production burdens. Apply the same boundary discipline to chip manufacture and chip use.

Carbon is one indicator

A climate result does not cover every environmental effect. Use carbon to find important contributions, while keeping other relevant impacts visible in the case assessment.
