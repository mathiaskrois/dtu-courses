Topics

Better does not necessarily mean sustainable

Relative assessment asks which option has lower impact. Absolute assessment compares impact with an allocated share of environmental capacity; both options can improve while still exceeding that share.

Allocation is a consequential choice

Translating a planetary boundary to a product or activity requires a sharing principle. Human needs, population or other allocation choices imply different ethical assumptions and can change the result.

Compare like units and scales

A footprint and its allocated boundary must describe compatible quantities, time periods and indicators. The ratio is only useful once those choices are explicit.
