Topics

Make the assessment answer one question

Microfabrication offers several possible projects: a manufacturer, DTU’s facility, a specific process or high-purity chemicals. Use your group’s selected topic and keep the assessment question narrow enough to investigate.

Missing data is part of the case

This is an investigative project rather than a textbook dataset. Record proxies, gaps and assumptions as you go; an apparently precise number is weak evidence if its inputs are poorly supported.

Task 1 is the assessment contract

The problem statement, functional unit and boundaries set what later calculations mean. A clear scope now reduces rework when you quantify impacts in Task 2.

FOR YOUR MICROFABRICATION PROJECT

Use the case session to clarify scope. Task 1 is due on 30 September; the session itself is not listed as a graded submission.

Stress-test your problem statement

Optional practice

Audit your current Microfabrication problem statement. Record the scope, the motivations and the information you still need.
