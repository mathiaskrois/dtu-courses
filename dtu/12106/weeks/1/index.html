Topics

Sustainability is a constraint on engineering decisions

A lower footprint is useful, but planetary thresholds ask whether the total activity remains within environmental limits. Keep the scale of the system visible when making a sustainability claim.

Assess all three pillars

Environmental improvements can create economic or social trade-offs. For microfabrication, cheap or reliable chips alone do not establish sustainability; resource use and impacts also belong in the problem definition.

Commitments influence what counts as progress

Global commitments and the EU taxonomy provide context for engineering choices. Distinguish an evidence-based assessment from an unsupported green claim.
