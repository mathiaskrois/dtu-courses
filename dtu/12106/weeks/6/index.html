Topics

Purchase price is not life-cycle cost

Life Cycle Costing includes costs over the chosen lifetime and boundary. An expensive efficient option can have lower total cost; record usage and price assumptions before comparing.

Sensitivity reveals decision drivers

Changing electricity prices or lifetime can change which option is cheapest. Test influential assumptions rather than treating a single price scenario as a permanent answer.

MCDA makes trade-offs explicit

Multi-Criteria Decision Assessment and AHP combine criteria using preferences or weights. The ranking depends on those choices, so explain them and check how robust the conclusion is.

Cost is one pillar, not the whole conclusion

A low-cost process can still have environmental or social burdens. For microfabrication, place cost results alongside the other dimensions instead of replacing them.
