01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

The central idea

Interaction data can support design decisions only when the measurement is linked to a meaningful user outcome. Avoid treating every collected event as useful evidence.

02

Make the assumptions visible

State what your example assumes, what information is available, and what would change the conclusion. Use the linked course material to check your reasoning.

03

Keep evidence with the result

Record a small example, its expected outcome and one limitation. A result is easier to trust when someone else can follow how you obtained it.

02 / PRACTISE

Try it: Data-driven design

30–60 min · Suggested

Choose two metrics for a fictional app. Explain one behavior each metric captures and one important outcome it misses.
