01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

The central idea

A visualization supports a user's decision when its encodings, context and interactions match that decision. Dense information needs a clear hierarchy and a route to detail.

02

Make the assumptions visible

State what your example assumes, what information is available, and what would change the conclusion. Use the linked course material to check your reasoning.

03

Keep evidence with the result

Record a small example, its expected outcome and one limitation. A result is easier to trust when someone else can follow how you obtained it.

02 / PRACTISE

Try it: UX and visualizations

30–60 min · Suggested

Sketch a fictional clinical dashboard with one overview and one detail view. Explain which question each view answers without interpreting real patient data.
