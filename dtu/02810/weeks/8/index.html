01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

The central idea

Contextual interfaces can be helpful while also creating privacy, persuasion and accessibility concerns. Make their effects visible and preserve user control.

02

Make the assumptions visible

State what your example assumes, what information is available, and what would change the conclusion. Use the linked course material to check your reasoning.

03

Keep evidence with the result

Record a small example, its expected outcome and one limitation. A result is easier to trust when someone else can follow how you obtained it.

02 / PRACTISE

Try it: Wearables and ethics

30–60 min · Suggested

Describe one beneficial and one potentially manipulative wearable interaction. Propose a design change that gives the user meaningful control.
