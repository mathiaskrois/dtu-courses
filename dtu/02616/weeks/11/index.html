01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

A communicator defines the participants

A subgroup can communicate independently of other ranks. Rank numbers are local to the communicator, so world and subgroup ranks can differ.

02

Collectives use communicator membership

Every required member of that communicator must call compatible collectives. A subgroup collective is not automatically a world-wide operation.

03

Separation can clarify program roles

Different rank groups may perform different stages. Explain why this organisation helps the application instead of adding groups without a purpose.

02 / PRACTISE

Trace a communicator split

30–60 min · Suggested

World ranks 0–5 split by colour=rank mod 2 and use the world rank as key. List the two groups and each member’s new local rank. Which ranks participate in a collective on the odd group?
