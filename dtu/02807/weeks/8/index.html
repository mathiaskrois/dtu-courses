01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

Make the question evaluable

Define the problem and what a successful result would look like. A small, well-evaluated system is easier to finish than an open-ended collection of algorithms.

02

Choose substantial techniques

At least one qualifying course topic must be central; two are preferred. Also name a method not directly taught and explain its distinct purpose.

03

Check data and team feasibility

Group size must be four or five. Confirm access to suitable data and divide responsibilities before committing to an oversized scope.
