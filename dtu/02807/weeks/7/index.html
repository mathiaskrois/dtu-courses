Topics

Betweenness identifies potential bridges

Edge betweenness counts shortest-path traffic. Girvan–Newman removes high-betweenness edges to reveal components; an expensive centrality calculation may limit its scale.

Modularity compares observed and expected edges

A partition’s score compares within-community edges with a degree-based null model. A high score alone does not prove the communities are meaningful for the application.

Compare algorithms on the same graph

Louvain seeks modularity improvements; spectral approaches use graph matrices and eigenvectors. Graph construction and weights can matter as much as the community-detection routine.

Separate two linked triangles

Optional practice

The undirected graph has edges AB, BC, CA, DE, EF, FD, CD. Split it into {A,B,C} and {D,E,F}. Identify the bridge and compute Q=Σ[Lc/m−(Dc/(2m))²].
