01 / UNDERSTAND

What matters this week

01

Algorithms encode different cluster shapes

K-means uses distances to centroids and requires k. Hierarchical methods produce a merge structure. DBSCAN uses density and can identify noise; these assumptions lead to different results.

02

Representation determines the distances

Feature scales and irrelevant variables can dominate a distance calculation. Explain preprocessing and inspect plots rather than interpreting cluster labels as ground truth.

03

One metric is not a complete evaluation

Davies–Bouldin rewards compact, separated clusters in its distance setting. Compare its result with the problem, visual structure and sensitivity to parameters.

02 / PRACTISE

Do one k-means step

30–60 min · Suggested

Points are 0, 1, 9, 10 with initial centres 0 and 10. Assign every point to its nearest centre, update the centres, and compute within-cluster sum of squared distances. Then explain one limitation of this example.
