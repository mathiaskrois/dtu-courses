Data mining & TF.IDF

Topics

A pattern needs to be useful and valid

A large dataset produces many apparently interesting coincidences. Ask whether the result generalises and matters to a real decision, rather than treating a detected pattern as proof.

TF.IDF combines local frequency and global rarity

The lecture normalises a term count by the largest term count in that document, then multiplies by log₂(N/df). Common terms across the whole collection have little discriminating weight.

Define the question before selecting a tool

Descriptive and predictive tasks need different evidence. The final project must answer a relevant question with a substantial course algorithm; introductory TF.IDF alone does not satisfy the course-topic requirement.

Calculate a term score and a false-match expectation

Optional practice

A collection contains 1,024 documents. A term appears in 8 documents, occurs 3 times in this document, and its most frequent term occurs 12 times. Compute TF, IDF and their product. Then consider 10,000 random tests with false-match probability 0.01 each.
