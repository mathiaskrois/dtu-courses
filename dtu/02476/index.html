WEEK 01 / 2026-01-05

Development, versioning and reproducibility

An experiment is reproducible only when its code, data, environment and configuration can be identified together. Logging helps connect a result to the exact choices that produced it.

Open week 1 page ↗Week page ↗
01

Understand

The essential ideas
  1. The central idea

    An experiment is reproducible only when its code, data, environment and configuration can be identified together. Logging helps connect a result to the exact choices that produced it.

  2. Make the assumptions visible

    State what your example assumes, what information is available, and what would change the conclusion. Use the linked course material to check your reasoning.

  3. Keep evidence with the result

    Record a small example, its expected outcome and one limitation. A result is easier to trust when someone else can follow how you obtained it.
